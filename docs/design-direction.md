# Portfolio design direction

Reading this as: a developer portfolio for recruiters, with a confident editorial tech language that foregrounds real shipped work over inflated claims.

- Design variance: 6/10
- Motion intensity: 5/10
- Visual density: 4/10
- Foundation: custom Tailwind v4 portfolio composition with a graphite, warm-white, and cobalt visual system.
- Layout: asymmetric editorial hero; project index; skills ribbon; credential block; contact close.
- Motion: restrained page-load sequencing and project-card hover movement. Reduced motion is respected.
- Content policy: claims are limited to linked or user-provided evidence. No fabricated metrics, job history, or client outcomes.
